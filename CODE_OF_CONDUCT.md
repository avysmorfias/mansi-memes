# Code of Conduct
## Our goal
Mansi Memes is a small open-source project about the Mansi language and its use in modern Internet culture.

We want the project to be a friendly place for people interested in the Mansi language, languages in general, memes, and linguistic diversity.

Contributions, corrections, questions, and disagreements are welcome. They should be handled with respect.
## Expected behavior
Participants are expected to:
* be respectful and considerate towards others;
* discuss ideas, language material, and mistakes without attacking people;
* accept reasonable corrections and constructive criticism;
* give others a chance to explain their position;
* respect different levels of knowledge and experience;
* provide sources or explanations when making claims about Mansi material, whenever possible;
* respect the people, communities, and languages represented in the project.
## Unacceptable behavior
The following behavior is not acceptable:

* harassment, threats, or personal attacks;
* discrimination or hateful behavior towards people or groups;
* deliberately insulting or humiliating other participants;
* repeated unwanted contact or personal targeting;
* deliberately disrupting discussions or contributions;
* publishing someone's private information without permission;
* any other behavior that would make participation in the project unsafe or hostile.

Disagreements about the Mansi language, its varieties, translations, sources, or other project content are allowed. Such disagreements should remain focused on the subject rather than becoming personal conflicts.
## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to the Mansi Memes repository, its Issues, Pull Requests, discussions, and other project-related spaces.

It also applies when a participant is representing the project or its community in other public spaces.
## Enforcement
If you believe that someone has violated this Code of Conduct, please report the situation to the repository maintainer through GitHub or another contact method listed in the repository.

Reports should include enough information to understand what happened and, when possible, links or other relevant context.

The maintainer will review reports and decide what action is appropriate. Depending on the situation, actions may include asking someone to stop the behavior, removing inappropriate content, closing an Issue or Pull Request, or restricting participation in the project.

Reports will be handled as privately as reasonably possible.
## Acknowledgement
This Code of Conduct is intended to help keep Mansi Memes a welcoming and constructive project.

Thank you for helping make the Mansi language a little more visible on the Internet.
